Teaching approach

  • Active learning. Short questions, polls, live coding, and guided exercises make students’ reasoning visible and help me adjust the pace of a class.
  • Structured practice. Incremental assignments begin with a working foundation and build toward deeper understanding. Students practice Git, testing, debugging, and clear documentation.
  • Useful feedback. Clear rubrics distinguish correctness, design, and communication. Frequent checkpoints give students time to apply feedback before major assessments.
  • An accessible classroom. Consistent policies, clear expectations, accommodations, and multiple ways to ask for help support students with different backgrounds and preparation.
