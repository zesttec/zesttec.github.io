About Dongfang Zhao

I am a PhD candidate in Computer Science at Indiana University Bloomington, advised by Xiaofeng Wang and Haixu Tang. I also work closely with Hang Zhang.

My research interests are system security and privacy, applied machine learning, and software engineering.
